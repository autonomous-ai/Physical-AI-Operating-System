# Stretch break

When you have been at your desk for about fifty minutes without getting up,
the lamp looks at you, does a slow stretch of its own and warms its light for
a few seconds: an invitation to get up too. It does not speak, and it does
not do it again while you stay; once you have stepped away and come back, it
starts counting again.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When you've been sitting a
long time I stretch, as a hint to take a break. I won't keep at it. Tell me to
stop if you'd rather I didn't."
