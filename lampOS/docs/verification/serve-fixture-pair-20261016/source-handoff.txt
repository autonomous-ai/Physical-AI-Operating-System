Measurement interpretation
The source retains all four host identities in an unfiltered serve_session run. This audit proves registration and full invocation, not that those subprocesses have run or passed. A passing unfiltered serial binary run with all 14 identities and zero ignored/filtered tests establishes all 22 attempts; a failing identity may stop its remaining loop/second/third attempts, so 14 test result lines alone do not prove 22 completed attempts.
A before/after binary measurement must include the four host cases on both sides and keep compilation outside the execution boundary. It may honestly describe 10/14 identities and 18/22 attempts selecting coordinated time, while reporting full-binary elapsed time separately. A clock-only subset cannot be labeled full-binary time.
