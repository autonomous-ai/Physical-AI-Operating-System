# Focus timer

You say "focus for 25 minutes" and the lamp's voice sets a 25-minute timer.
When the timer runs out and you are at the desk, the lamp turns to you and
nods once, alongside the voice telling you the time is up.

What it cannot do yet, as asked:

- **The calm light for the whole focus.** A skill may change the light only
  for a short while (at most ten minutes) and always gives your light back;
  nothing can hold it dimmed for 25 minutes. If you want it calmer while you
  focus, ask the lamp to dim the light yourself when you start.
- **Knowing which timer it is.** The lamp is told only that a timer or
  reminder is due, not which one, so it nods at the end of any timer or
  reminder, not only a focus one.
- **Starting the timer.** The voice sets the timer when you ask; this skill
  does not hear the words. It only answers the end.

It nods only if someone is at the desk to see it, once per timer, and not
again within two minutes. Until the lamp's pause gate is built, it would nod
even while the lamp is paused.

If asked about it: "When your timer runs out, I look at you and nod. Tell me
if you'd rather I didn't, and it can be taken out."
