# Stretch break, the demo

**A demo:** the real Stretch break (`../stretch-break`) with a minute in
place of fifty, and five minutes between two, so a person sees it at once.
Install one or the other, not both.

When you have been at your desk for about a minute (fifty, in the real one) without getting up,
the lamp looks at you and does a slow stretch of its own: an invitation to get
up too. (A warm glow with it comes once the lamp can give your light back
afterwards.) It does not speak, and it does
not do it again while you stay; once you have stepped away and come back, it
starts counting again.

If asked about it, the lamp says something like: "When you've been sitting a
long time I stretch, as a hint to take a break. I won't keep at it. If you'd rather
I didn't, say so and it can be taken out."
