## Schedule: source and reuse review (draft, 2026-10-14)

V1's verdict is `rebuild from the idea` (GO-3.19). Its `system/schedule` sources and 108 authored top-level tests provide useful cases for strict occurrence boundaries, weekday/month-end recurrence, DST day uniqueness, busy deferral, failed-handoff retry, and honest handoff reporting. Its one-minute polling, five-minute repeat floor, per-device ±five-minute jitter, and discarding occurrences over 30 minutes late served a backend-controlled prompt scheduler; they do not establish suitable behavior for a person's short timer or task 6's promise to retain missed reminders. The file replacement is atomic but not synced, and speech is handed off before the result is saved. Retain the useful cases and explicit outcome distinctions; do not infer power-loss durability or heard-once delivery from them. Exact source references are in `/private/tmp/lamp-third-schedule-source-review.md`.

Apple's published HomePod interface supports several named timers, asking what remains or what is running, explicit pause/resume, and alarm snoozing. Those are useful wording and control references; they do not document a lamp-compatible restart or missed-delivery guarantee. The takeaway is identifiable timers and explicit control over them, with supported operations stated plainly. [Apple HomePod timer and alarm guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/homepod/use-alarms-clocks-and-timers-apd82db5cb0d/1.0/1.0).

The workspace already locks Jiff 0.2.38. Its timezone API supports IANA zones and TZif data, and a fallible system-zone lookup; its explicit disambiguation policies can reject nonexistent or repeated civil times instead of guessing. This is a maintained basis for date conversion, unlike copying Voice's current-offset formatter into a future-date scheduler. A product choice about a DST gap/fold remains explicit: Jiff's default selects after a gap and the earlier occurrence in a fold, while `Reject` reports ambiguity. [Jiff TimeZone](https://docs.rs/jiff/0.2.38/jiff/tz/struct.TimeZone.html), [Jiff Disambiguation](https://docs.rs/jiff/0.2.38/jiff/tz/enum.Disambiguation.html).

For delivery, reuse the typed bus and private JSON-line component boundary, but keep pending work in Schedule's own durable store. `schedule/due` is a bounded metadata-only discovery value with a stable store/item/delivery identity, not a speech permission or acknowledgement. First's Voice integration must retrieve that exact occurrence privately and acknowledge only confirmed completion, retaining uncertainty when an output receipt is missing.

## What this review has not shown

The Apple and Jiff statements are published source claims, rechecked during this task, not experiments. This draft does not claim other assistants preserve missed alarms across power loss, settle the complete Schedule product/API choices, or qualify lamp clock accuracy, flash durability, privacy integration, acoustic completion, or due-to-speech latency. Only the separate message-contract tests have been run by this agent; they do not establish service behavior. Google timer documentation returned HTTP 429 on direct retrieval and supplies no additional claim here.
